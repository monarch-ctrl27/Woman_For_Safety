The Woman For Safety Architecture & Mission

"Woman For Safety is a crowd-sourced early-warning system paired with a hands-free panic button, built for the moment a woman needs both at once."

1. Community-Crowdsourced Live Safety Map

Women know which streets have broken lights, which pedestrian paths have regular loitering, and which corners are notorious for violent muggings or harassment. Woman For Safety aggregates this lived knowledge into an active, community-verified map. Every time a woman navigates through the city, the routing engine queries these danger circles and computes automatic detours.

2. 60-Second Stand-Down Escalation Protocol

When entering within 600m of a flagged danger area, the device pulses vibration and initiates a 60-second stand-down window. If the user is safe, she can cancel it using her 4-digit secret Safe PIN. If 60 seconds expire without disarming, the system escalates automatically — protecting a victim even if her phone has been grabbed or knocked away.

3. Zero-Unlock Hands-Free Triggers

In an active attack, there is no time to unlock a screen or navigate an app interface. Woman For Safety arms 3 hands-free channels:

  • Triple-Press Hardware Event: Rapid 3-tap cadence on device buttons immediately triggers emergency dispatch.
  • Voice Distress Keyword: Continuous background listening for "Help" or "Emergency".
  • Accelerometer Fall Detection: Sensor detection of free-fall followed by severe physical impact.

4. Always Know Where to Go

Woman For Safety doesn't only warn what to avoid — it maintains live coordinates and 1-tap navigation to accredited safe sanctuaries: SAPS police stations, 24-hour trauma casualty units, and women's safe shelters.
