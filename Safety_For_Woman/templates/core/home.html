SafeHer Operations Platform

Admin, Moderation & Simulation Hub

This web platform is the operational command center serving administrators, community safety moderators, and institutional partners (universities, student residences, and Business Improvement Districts). The mobile end-user application is built separately in Flutter.

⚠️ Per SRS v1.2 (§2.8 & FR-PANIC-1): Emergency panic escalations dispatch to Next-of-Kin ONLY. No direct or automatic police dispatch is claimed or executed. There is no panic button on this operations web console.

🛡️

Community Danger Moderation

Moderators review crowd-sourced pins submitted by women across key metro belts (Westdene, Willows, Universitas, Brandwag, CBD). Triage unverified pins, calibrate detour radii, and enforce safety audit compliance per SRS §3.7.

⚡

Panic Pipeline Simulation

Demonstrates the emergency pipeline without endangering end-users. Inspect zero-unlock trigger logs (Voice 'Help', Triple-Press, Fall Detection, Geofence Stand-Down) and verify Next-of-Kin notifications.

🏥

Safe Havens & Sanctuaries Directory

Curate verified 24-hour SAPS desks, trauma casualty units, and accredited women's shelters. Supply direct routing coordinates and emergency hotlines to ensure women always know where to run, not just what to avoid.

🏢

Institutional Partnerships

Institutional accounts provide enterprise coverage for university campus safety directorates, accredited student residences, and Business Improvement Districts (BIDs) with corridor-level heatmaps and SLA reporting.

Looking for the SafeHer Mobile App?

The end-user mobile client is built natively in Flutter for iOS and Android, featuring background sensors, physical key hooks, and offline routing. This web console is restricted to operational management.
